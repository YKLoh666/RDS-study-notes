- $I_{entropy,full} = -\frac{8}{14} \log_2 \frac{8}{14} - \frac{6}{14} \log_2 \frac{6}{14} = 0.9852$
- Check information gain of splitting `fbs`
  - $I_{entropy,fbs=0} = -\frac{6}{7} \log_2 \frac{6}{7} - \frac{1}{7} \log_2 \frac{1}{7} = 0.5917$
  - $I_{entropy,fbs=1} = -\frac{2}{7} \log_2 \frac{2}{7} - \frac{5}{7} \log_2 \frac{5}{7} = 0.8631$
  - $IG(fbs) = 0.9852 - \left(\frac{7}{14} \times 0.5917 + \frac{7}{14} \times 0.8631\right) = 0.2578$
- Check information gain of splitting `thal`
  - $I_{entropy,thal=3} = -\frac{1}{4} \log_2 \frac{1}{4} - \frac{3}{4} \log_2 \frac{3}{4} = 0.8113$
  - $I_{entropy,thal=6} = -\frac{4}{6} \log_2 \frac{4}{6} - \frac{2}{6} \log_2 \frac{2}{6} = 0.9183$
  - $I_{entropy,thal=7} = -\frac{3}{4} \log_2 \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} \log_2 \frac{1}{4} = 0.8113$
  - $IG(thal) = 0.9852 - \left(\frac{4}{14} \times 0.8113 + \frac{6}{14} \times 0.9183 + \frac{4}{14} \times 0.8113\right) = 0.1280$
- Since `fbs` has higher information gain, it will be chosen as the root node.
